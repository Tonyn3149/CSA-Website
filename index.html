What is CSA?

CSA is for student who want to connect over Chinese culture, food, and traditions — whether you grew up with it or are just curious to learn. We host events all year long, from festival celebrations to game nights, and enriches the vibrant campus life at UTD.

  • Celebrate festivals like Lunar New Year and Mid-Autumn Festival together.
  • Step out of your comfort zone and meet fellow students in a relaxed, welcoming setting.
  • Whether you're deeply rooted in the culture or just eager to learn.
